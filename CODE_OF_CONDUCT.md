Code of Conduct
Our Commitment

We are committed to maintaining a professional, inclusive, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone who participates in this project.

This repository is an open-source learning and development project focused on Machine Learning, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning, NLP, Computer Vision, MLOps, and related technologies.

We welcome contributions from people of all experience levels and backgrounds.

Expected Behavior

All contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to:

Communicate respectfully and professionally.
Be constructive when providing feedback or reviewing contributions.
Focus on technical discussions and project objectives.
Respect different perspectives, backgrounds, and levels of experience.
Give appropriate credit to original authors and sources.
Follow applicable licenses and intellectual property requirements.
Keep discussions relevant to the project.
Act in good faith when submitting issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions.
Help maintain a welcoming and productive open-source community.
Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
Hate speech or derogatory comments targeting individuals or groups.
Threats, violence, or encouragement of harmful behavior.
Sexual harassment or inappropriate sexual content.
Publishing private or personally identifiable information without consent.
Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated bad-faith participation.
Spam, malicious content, or unrelated promotional activity.
Plagiarism or misrepresentation of another person's work.
Intentionally introducing malicious, destructive, or harmful code.
Any conduct that is inappropriate for a professional open-source environment.
Technical Discussions

Technical disagreement is a normal and valuable part of open-source development.

Contributors should:

Support technical opinions with reasoning, documentation, experiments, or other relevant evidence.
Critique implementations and ideas rather than individuals.
Remain open to alternative approaches.
Keep discussions professional and focused on the project's goals.

Disagreement should never become personal.

Contribution Standards

Contributions should:

Follow the repository's existing structure and conventions.
Include appropriate documentation where necessary.
Maintain a reasonable standard of code quality.
Avoid unnecessary breaking changes.
Respect existing licenses and attribution requirements.
Avoid committing credentials, secrets, private data, or confidential information.
Clearly identify experimental or incomplete implementations.
Include relevant tests or validation where appropriate.
Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it privately to the project maintainer through the appropriate GitHub communication channel.

When possible, include:

A description of the incident.
Relevant links, commits, issues, or pull requests.
The date and context of the incident.
Any other information that may assist with reviewing the report.

Please do not publicly disclose sensitive or personal information.

Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the severity and circumstances of a violation, maintainers may take one or more of the following actions:

Request corrective behavior.
Remove inappropriate content.
Close issues, discussions, or pull requests.
Restrict participation temporarily.
Permanently restrict participation in the project.

Enforcement decisions will be made based on the circumstances of each case and with the goal of maintaining a professional and productive project environment.

Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers are expected to:

Apply project standards consistently.
Review reported violations responsibly.
Protect the privacy of individuals involved in reports.
Encourage constructive technical collaboration.
Take appropriate action when project standards are violated.
Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project-related spaces, including:

GitHub Issues
Pull Requests
Discussions
Code Reviews
Repository Contributions
Documentation
Project-related communication

It also applies whenever an individual is representing the project or participating in an official project activity.
